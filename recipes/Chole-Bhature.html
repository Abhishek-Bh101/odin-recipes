Chole Bhature

How to make Chole Bhature

Chole Bhature needs an overnight preparation because of the chickpeas so it should be made only when time can be given.

Ingredients

  • Chickpeas
  • Flour
  • Chole Masala

Steps

  1. Soak the chickpeas overnight.
  2. Cook the chickpeas in a pressure cooker with all the masalas.
  3. Knead the dough and fry the bhaturas, Chole-Bhature are ready.
