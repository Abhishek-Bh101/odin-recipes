Dal Makhani

How to Make Dal Makhani

The recipe is a restaurant style recipe that should be made using the finest ingredients possible.

Ingredients

  • Lentils
  • Cream
  • Fenugreek Leaves

Steps

  1. Soak Lentils
  2. Pressure Cook after soaking them.
  3. Make Masala and saute in the dal.
  4. Slow cook the dal for as long as possible, preferably 24 hours.
