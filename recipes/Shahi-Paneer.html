How to make Shahi Paneer

This Recipe can be made both in home and restaurants alile, Fresh Ingredients are preferable.

Ingredients

  • Paneer
  • Onions
  • Tomatos
  • Cream

Steps

  1. Make a thin paste of tomatos and onions.
  2. Put Paneer in after cooking and putting the required spices in the paste, add water if required and then cook for a few more minutes.
